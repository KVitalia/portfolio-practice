## Type

Two families: **Istok Web** (`font-istok`, weight 700 — wordmark only) and
**Wix Madefor Text** (`font-sans`, the default — everything else). Both support Cyrillic.
Letter-spacing is `0` across the whole scale.

This scale condenses the many one-off sizes from the first draft (11, 14, 18, 24, 26, 28, 36,
40, 48, 64px) into five steps. Where a specific component still needs an in-between size at a
breakpoint, step up to the next rung rather than introducing a new value.
