## 1. Positioning (one sentence)

I research how customers choose a small business, then structure the website's content and flow around that decision, so owners convert more visitors into customers — bookings, foot traffic, or online enquiries alike.

**Hero headline:** Design built on how people choose.

**Description:** I research how people decide to choose a small business, then structure the site around that decision, so owners convert more visitors into customers — bookings, foot traffic, or online enquiries alike.

**Site name:** Vitalia Kumeska — use in the About page, footer, and contact details.

**Voice:** Professional but conversational — clear enough for a customer to follow, without heavy design jargon.

## 3. Home page blocks, in order

1. **Hero** — a bold, large headline stating what you do (not just a job title), paired with the one-sentence positioning underneath. Optionally, one or two key words in the headline carry a hover note explaining what you mean by them (the Chaachie Designs touch) — this needs you to pick which words and what the note says.
2. **Short intro paragraph** — a few sentences, Matt Fredette-style: who you are and the throughline in your work (researching how people decide, then designing around it), stated plainly rather than sold.
3. **Featured work** — preview cards for your 3–4 projects, each with a one-line hook of what was solved, linking to its full case study.
4. **Services (optional)** — structured the way olhalazarieva.com does it: a short, scannable list of what you do (e.g. research, UX flows, UI design, dashboards) rather than a paragraph.
5. **About teaser** — one or two sentences + photo, linking through to the full About page.
6. **Contact / CTA** — a direct closing line inviting a project conversation, with your contact method.
